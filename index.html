Common Questions

You Asked, We Answered

Will I get a fair price for my house?

Yes. Each offer is calculated based on your property's current condition, its location, and prevailing market values, less the anticipated costs of repairs, holding time, and resale. Our team reviews these factors transparently with you and welcomes any questions regarding how a specific offer was determined.

Do I have to fix anything before selling?

Not at all. Our acquisitions team purchases properties in any condition, ranging from move-in ready homes to those requiring substantial renovation. You are never required to clean, repair, or improve the property prior to sale.

How fast can we actually close?

In many cases, our team can complete a closing in as little as two weeks. That said, we remain flexible and are pleased to accommodate a longer timeline should your circumstances require additional time.

Am I obligated to accept the offer?

You are under no obligation whatsoever. Both the initial consultation and the resulting offer are provided free of charge, and you are welcome to take the time you need to consider your options or decline entirely.

What if I'm already in foreclosure?

Timing is critical in these circumstances. We encourage you to contact our team as early as possible; in many cases, we are able to move quickly enough to help homeowners avoid foreclosure altogether.

Ready to Talk It Through?

There is no cost and no obligation associated with requesting your offer. Our team is prepared to answer your questions and guide you through every step of the process — the only step remaining is to reach out.
